ReVora Quick Guide

Architecture, Scenarios, and API Workflow

⚡ Autonomous Recovery Cycle

The platform operates as a deterministic, bounded multi-agent state machine that transforms failed payment events into recovered revenue without human intervention.

STEP 1: Real-Time Ingestion & Risk Detection

Parses Stripe, Razorpay, and ERP webhooks. Distinguishes recoverable soft declines from terminal card theft or fraud.

STEP 2: Root-Cause Reasoning Engine

Evaluates customer liquidity, historical payment timings, and churn signals to formulate targeted recovery plans and multilingual copy.

STEP 3: Policy & Compliance Guardrail Gate

Enforces TCPA quiet hours, contact fatigue limits (≤ 3 per 7 days), and the immediate DISPUTE_KILLSWITCH.

STEP 4: Bounded Action Execution

Executes smart gateway retries, sends dynamic discount payment links, or dispatches conversational voice agents.

STEP 5: Immutable Cryptographic Ledger

Appends every recovered cent, gateway fee, and AI token cost to a SHA-256 hash-chained ledger for verified ROI calculation.
